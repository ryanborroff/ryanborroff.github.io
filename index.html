Selected work

Professional work and independent products
01 – Expedia Group

Designing clearer GenAI travel recommendations

Working with machine learning and data science teams, I designed and refined prompts and product language for personalised travel recommendations. The challenge was to make generated suggestions relevant and useful while keeping the experience clear and trustworthy.

Result: Testing and analysis showed that my content changes delivered significant improvements in conversion.

AI content designPrompt engineeringWeb & app

Content decisions

I worked on how recommendations were introduced and explained, including labels such as “You were interested in this property”, “Extra savings just for you” and “Highlights for your 4-night trip”. The aim was to explain why a suggestion was relevant without making personalisation feel intrusive.

02 – Qatar Airways

Content design across the Qatar Airways digital experience

Embedded in the UX team, I led mobile content design and worked directly with the client across the design process. My work covered journeys, UX writing and microcopy, helping customers navigate booking, trip management and loyalty features.

Role: Lead Content Designer / UX Writer

UX writingMicrocopyMobile app

Content decisions

I wrote clear prompts at important decision points, including “Let’s start your trip”, “Don’t see your trip? Add your booking” and “Avios are almost yours”. The language needed to help people move forward while remaining consistent with the airline’s brand voice.

03 – Chroma

A filmmaker-first platform for showing and sharing work

Chroma explores a more focused home for filmmakers: portfolios, video hosting and private client review, with wider distribution tools in the product vision. My work spans product definition, interface and content design, development and infrastructure, informed by beta feedback.

04 – Kitchen Wizz

Your recipes, organised and ready to cook

Kitchen Wizz brings saved recipes into one searchable place. Add a recipe from a link, photo or text, adjust quantities and measurements, plan meals and build a grocery list. I designed the experience and developed the app, from information architecture to working product.

iOS app in development and testing

React Native / ExpoFirebaseGemini API

Content decisions

The content focuses on the cook’s task rather than the technology behind it: straightforward recipe instructions, recognisable categories, clear measurement conversions and simple meal-planning language.

05 – EatLog

Log a meal by saying it

EatLog is a voice-first food diary. Say what you ate and review estimated calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat and fibre alongside daily targets and water intake. I designed and built the experience around making food logging quicker and easier to understand.

Designed and built end-to-end

Voice inputData visualisationiOS

Content decisions

The interface puts everyday language first. People can describe a meal naturally, review the resulting entry and see nutrition figures alongside their daily targets without navigating a complicated food database.
